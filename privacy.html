Privacy Policy

Pigeonhole for iPhone · Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Pigeonhole app ("the app", "we") uses, and why. The short version: the app has no accounts, keeps your game progress on your phone, and shows ads from Google AdMob to players who haven't bought Pigeonhole Pro.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not run our own servers. We have no account system and do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, streaks and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Wikipedia

To load each day's puzzle, the app requests article information from Wikipedia's public API (operated by the Wikimedia Foundation). Like any web request, this shares your device's IP address and basic technical details with Wikimedia. It does not include any personal information about you. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your daily score is submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboard so the app can show your own rank. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app shows only your own rank and the number of players, never other players' names.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Players who buy Pigeonhole Pro see no ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate (coarse) location;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with;
  • device and app diagnostic information.

Your choices. The app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission once. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they won't be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads, and you can revisit that choice in the app. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Pigeonhole Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

Children

Pigeonhole is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
