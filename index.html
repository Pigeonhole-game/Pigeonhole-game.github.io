Pigeonhole

A daily guessing game for iPhone. You see the categories an article is filed under, and you guess the article. Three new rounds every day.

How to play

Each round starts with two category labels. Type a guess, or leave the box empty and tap Next to reveal another label. The fewer labels and wrong guesses you need, the more points you score, up to 1,000 per round and 3,000 per day. New rounds arrive at midnight UTC.

Support

When do new puzzles appear?
Every day at 00:00 UTC (6 PM US Central time in winter, 7 PM in summer).
How do I restore Pigeonhole Pro?
Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
Something isn't working?
